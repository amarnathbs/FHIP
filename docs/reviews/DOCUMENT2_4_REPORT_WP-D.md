## F26 Forecast reads the saved age
Root cause: the forecast engine already used `retirement_members.target_retirement_age` for Self, but the Forecast page never showed it and presented two blank boxes ("Retirement date", "Months until retirement, no date of birth on file") even when a date of birth and a saved age existed.
Fix: one pure resolver `lib/engines/forecast/retirementTiming.ts` is used by the run and by the page (`loadRetirementTimingForPage`), so the page states the age the run will use and where it came from ("Self: target retirement age 67 (saved on the Retirement page). About 16 years to go."), for Self and Spouse. The months fallback is shown only without a usable date of birth. An exact date that overrides the age is announced. A what-if age (per person) is labelled "this run only, not saved", is sent with the run and writes nothing. Each run records the age and its source. The Spouse's date of birth (household record) is now read for the Spouse leg. Reload reflects a changed saved age because the page and the run read it fresh.
Tests: `retirementForecastSavedTargetAge.test.ts` (22): Self, Spouse, different ages, missing DOB, missing target, updated target, what-if not persisted, exact date, markup.
Changed shared files: `lib/services/forecastData.ts` (retirement branch only), `lib/validation/forecast.ts`, `app/api/forecast/run/route.ts` (two optional fields), `lib/engines/forecast/retirementCalculator.ts` (explanation inputs gain two fields). Existing forecast tests (43) still pass.

## F25b Balance and contribution are separate actions
Design: a contribution is a flow with its own ledger, `public.retirement_contributions` (migration **0304**, additive): member, destination (a retirement account or the SMSF fund account), type (employer, salary sacrifice, personal, spouse, government), amount, frequency or date, currency, source (manual or payslip) with the payslip it came from, status (active, waiting for a destination, superseded, removed). RLS own rows; a trigger keeps the destination the same person's and the SMSF kind on the SMSF account only; a unique index makes one payslip one row.
UI: the account form is balance only (the contribution and frequency fields were removed; the six "(contribution amount, not a balance)" catalogue rows are no longer offered as new balance rows; rows already saved keep showing). A "Contributions" card has "+ Add contribution" and one history table (date, type, person, goes to, amount, source, counted or why not). Imported payroll contributions appear in the same history with "From your payslip (employer)". Figures an account already held are shown as "Recorded on the account" and are never hidden or doubled.
Exactly once: the account keeps the two contribution figures every consumer already reads (Dashboard, Health Score, Twin, Forecast, SMSF reports); they are now DERIVED from the ledger (`runRate.ts`): the latest record of a series counts, one-offs are history, an unknown frequency is left out, an employer-less manual record is the same flow as the single payslip series of that account and type. A statement Apply (which writes those columns) is re-derived so two figures never stand for one contribution, and the person is told.
Tests: `retirementManualBalanceVsContribution.test.ts` (8), `retirementContributionRunRate.test.ts` (12), `retirementContributionService.test.ts` (20), `retirementContributionsMigration0304Pglite.test.ts` (6: constraints, trigger, unique index, idempotence, two-user RLS, negative control).
Migration: **0304** (my range 0304-0307; 0305-0307 unused). Rescan of origin/main found nothing above 0285. CHECK constraints widened: none (new table), so the drop-and-recreate trap does not apply.

## F25 Payslip employer super
Root causes: the payslip line `SGC Employer Cont. THE TRUSTEE FOR THE BEKAL SUPER FUND` was not recognised as employer super (it showed as "For information"), and no destination model existed.
Fix: AU label rules now read `sgc` and `employer cont`. On payslip APPROVE (the Accept; never on upload) `recordPayslipContribution` records one employer-contribution row for that payslip: to the SMSF whose name matches, else the retirement account whose name matches, else it waits for the person ("FHIP could not tell ... has not guessed and has not counted it yet" with a chooser and a disabled-until-chosen button, a plain message if no account in that currency exists). Matching is strict: whole-word identity of every distinctive word of the destination name (generic words such as super, fund, trustee, industry are ignored; a name made only of generic words never matches), no fuzzy or partial match, employer-name words cannot supply the match, Self's payslip never goes to a Spouse's account and the reverse (joint allowed), currency respected, two matches means ask, no "only fund" default. Idempotent: the same payslip uploaded or approved twice adds nothing; a revised payslip supersedes the earlier row; another person's payslip or account is a 404-style refusal. The payslip screen says what happened and links to Retirement.
Tests: `retirementContributionRouting.test.ts` (13), service tests (payslip rows above).

### What remains for the SMSF destination (WP-C dependency, stated clearly)
Built here against the existing structures, with a small interface (`SmsfDestinationProvider` in `lib/retirement-contributions/service.ts`; the default reads `smsf_funds`): an SMSF fund is a destination; a contribution to it is stored in the ledger with destination kind `smsf`, and the fund account's two contribution columns are derived from it, which is exactly what the existing SMSF contribution reconciliation (`smsfContributions.ts`, SMSF reports and cash flow) already reads. So an SMSF contribution shows in the existing SMSF reports and contribution view without WP-C.
NOT built here and left to WP-C and the PO: (1) prepopulating the SMSF tab's own contribution entry from the ledger (WP-C owns the SMSF tab and its contribution read model; it should read `retirement_contributions` rows with `destination_kind = 'smsf'` and `status = 'active'`, and must not add the account column on top, they are the same flow); (2) the SMSF Detailed derivation of the Summary value; (3) a decision on the retirement corpus: by the PO's 16-10-2026 personal-basis decision the SMSF fund row and its contribution are NOT in the personal forecast (the existing LR-6 guard keeps SMSF-linked contributions out of the household contribution). The PO's sentence "reflect into SMSF contribution amount for calculations of retirement corpus" needs a decision on a family-level corpus where the SMSF balance and its contribution enter TOGETHER, once. Not decided by me.
