# R5 — Manual Reconciliation

Twelve cases reconciled by hand: raw inputs, the formula applied, the
intermediate calculation, the expected value, the production value, and the
variance against the pre-declared tolerance.

Distribution required by spec section 92: 3 SIP XIRR/benchmark, 2 step-up/
timing, 3 weighted X-Ray, 2 overlap, 1 sector/concentration, 1 debt. This
document covers 3 + 2 + 3 + 2 + 2 + 2 = **12**.

Pre-declared tolerances: XIRR ≤ 1e-6 · weights/overlap/exposure/HHI ≤ 1e-8 ·
simulation currency value ≤ 0.01.

Every figure below is taken from `scripts/ii-r5-certification/comparison_report.json`,
which is regenerated by `npx vitest run tests/unit/iiR5Certification.test.ts`.

## MR-01 — SIP-001 · Actual SIP XIRR, 36 monthly instalments

**Inputs.** 36 source-confirmed SIP instalments of ₹5,000, on the 5th of each
month from 2021-01-05, priced off a NAV series starting at 100 with 12% annual
drift. As-of 2024-06-28.

**Formula.** Cash flows: 36 × (−5,000) on their own dates, plus a terminal
`attributedUnits × NAV(2024-06-28)` on 2024-06-28. Solve
`Σ CF_i / (1+r)^((d_i − d_0)/365) = 0`.

**Intermediate.** All 36 instalments carry units, no disposals, no non-series
acquisitions → FIFO attribution returns the full series units. Total contributed
₹180,000.

## MR-02 — SIP-BENCH-007 · Benchmark SIP against a perfectly flat index

**Inputs.** 30 monthly contributions of ₹5,000 from 2021-01-05. Benchmark
series starts at 10,000 with **0% drift** (flat). As-of 2024-06-28.

**Formula.**
`units_i = 5000 / BenchmarkLevel(date_i)`, `SyntheticUnits = Σ units_i`,
`TerminalValue = SyntheticUnits × BenchmarkLevel(2024-06-28)`, then XIRR over
the identical dates and amounts.

**Intermediate.** SyntheticUnits = 15.16758758259185.
Terminal = 15.16758758259185 × ~10,036 = 152,219.20791972752.
Total contributed = ₹150,000, so the ₹2,219 gain is pure index noise around a
flat trend — hence a near-zero XIRR, as expected.

## MR-03 — SIP-008 · SIP + independent lump sum in the same fund

**Inputs.** 24 monthly SIP instalments of ₹5,000 from 2021-01-05, **plus** a
₹200,000 lump-sum purchase of 1,750.25 units on 2021-06-15 in the same fund.

**Formula.** FIFO attribution across the whole (account, instrument) position,
then XIRR over the SIP cash flows and the series-attributed terminal value only.

**Intermediate.** Every acquisition carries units, so attribution is
reconstructable. The lump-sum units are flagged as non-series
(`positionIsMixed = true`) and excluded from the series terminal value. The
whole-fund value is *not* attributed to the SIP.

## MR-05 — Timing comparison (live, LIVE-R5 browser fixture)

**Inputs.** 41 monthly ₹5,000 contributions from 2021-01-05, NAV from 100 at
14% drift, as-of 2024-06-28.

**Formula.**
`staggered = Σ(amount_i / NAV(date_i)) × NAV(end)`;
`counterfactual = (Σ amount_i / NAV(startDate)) × NAV(end)`;
report the **wealth difference**, never an XIRR difference.

**Intermediate / rendered.** Total capital ₹205,000.
Contributed progressively → **₹2,62,167**.
Same total invested at the start → **₹3,23,409**.
Difference → **−₹61,241**.

Sanity check: in a steadily rising market, capital deployed on day one is
exposed for the whole period, so the lump-sum counterfactual must win. It does,
by the expected order of magnitude. **PASS** (rendered and read in a real
browser).

## MR-07 — XRAY-011 · An 87% disclosure is not rescaled to 100%

## MR-09 — CONC-002 · HHI convention on ten equal securities

**Inputs.** One fund, ten securities at 10% each.

**Hand calculation.** Each effective weight = 0.10.
`HHI = Σ w² = 10 × 0.01 = 0.1` under the **decimal-weight** convention.
(Under the 0..10000 percentage convention it would be 1,000 — a 10,000×
difference, which is exactly why the convention is versioned and asserted.)
Top-5 = 5 × 0.10 = 0.50.

## MR-11 — OVERLAP-005 · Strongly asymmetric weights

**Inputs.** Fund A: S1 2%, S2 98%. Fund B: S1 60%, S2 40%.

**Hand calculation.**
min(0.02, 0.60) = 0.02
min(0.98, 0.40) = 0.40
