**Type:** Article | **Jurisdiction:** Global | **Risk class:** GREEN

**30-second answer / definition:** Financial health is the ability to manage today’s money needs, absorb a reasonable financial shock, make progress toward important goals and retain enough flexibility to make choices. It is broader than income, wealth or a single score. A household can have a high income and still be financially fragile, or a modest income and be financially well organised.

**Key takeaways:**
- Financial health is multidimensional: cash flow, resilience, debt, assets and progress toward goals all matter.
- Income and wealth are inputs, not complete answers. What matters is how sustainable the household’s overall position is.
- A useful financial-health check looks at both today and the future: can you meet current commitments, handle disruption and keep moving toward goals?
- Financial health changes over time. Regular measurement is more useful than treating one snapshot as a permanent label.
